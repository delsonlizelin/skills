Concrete remembers the afternoon

By late afternoon a dark roof can be far hotter than the air above it. Brick, stone and asphalt soak up that heat for hours, and they have a lot of it to give back once the sun goes down.

In open country the ground cools quickly into a clear sky. Downtown, tall walls block that view of the sky, so warmth bounces between buildings instead of escaping. The gap is widest a few hours after midnight, when people most need rest.

02 · Three things that cool a street

Shade that breathes

Canopy blocks the afternoon sun, and leaves release water into the air all evening.

Water that stays

Soil and ponds that hold rain cool as they dry, unlike sealed pavement that sheds it.

Open sky

Gaps between buildings let heat radiate upward rather than bounce between walls.
