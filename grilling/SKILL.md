# Grilling

Expose assumptions, consequential choices, failure modes, and missing completion criteria. Use the current request to select one mode; do not turn a plan request into an open-ended interview.

## Plan stress-test

When the user asks for a plan or enters plan mode, check the proposed approach against the objective, available evidence, dependencies, authority boundaries, and a proportionate verification and stopping condition. Explore facts in the permitted environment instead of asking the user to supply them.

Resolve routine choices from context. Ask one question at a time only when an unresolved decision materially changes the outcome or requires authorization. Give the recommendation and its trade-off with each question. Do not re-ask settled questions or require a separate confirmation of shared understanding merely to present the plan.

Finish when the plan is actionable and its material assumptions or unresolved decisions are explicit. A plan-only request does not authorize implementation; when the user has also authorized implementation, continue within that authorization after the stress-test.

## Explicit grill interview

When the user explicitly asks to be grilled, walk the consequential branches of the decision tree, resolving dependencies one at a time. Ask one question, provide a recommended answer and its trade-off, then wait for the user's response. Do not bundle questions.

Look up discoverable facts within scope. Put material decisions to the user; do not interrogate every routine implementation detail. Continue until the consequential choices are settled and the user confirms shared understanding, or explicitly ends the interview. Do not implement the idea during an interview unless the user authorizes that transition.
