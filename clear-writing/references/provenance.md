# Provenance and maintenance notes

This skill is an original synthesis of the user's writing principles and selectively adapted ideas from the following public projects. The wording and workflow here were rewritten in English for this local architecture.

- ReviewWrite by songhai-dg, MIT: https://github.com/songhai-dg/review-write
  - Adapted ideas: semantic preservation, protected facts and qualifiers, review before rewrite, and post-rewrite verification.
- Better Writing by forjd, MIT: https://github.com/forjd/better-writing
  - Adapted ideas: audience and purpose calibration, contextual style choices, pattern clusters rather than single-feature bans, and no-op behavior on good prose.
- Practical Prose by jlevy, code under MIT and content under CC BY 4.0: https://github.com/jlevy/practical-prose
  - Adapted ideas: separating purpose, expression, reasoning, grounding, and judgment; limiting automatic edits to safe prose changes.
- Good Writing by yelban, MIT: https://github.com/yelban/orz99-skills
- Writing Skills TW by aeopress, code under MIT and content under CC BY 4.0: https://github.com/aeopress/writing-skills.TW
  - Adapted ideas: stronger verbs, reduced nominalization, sentence-level Chinese readability, and conservative handling of technical prose. Mechanical sentence-length and rhythm thresholds were not adopted.
- Humanizer by blader, MIT: https://github.com/blader/humanizer
  - Adapted ideas: detect clusters of genuine machine-like patterns, preserve authorial voice, and avoid false positives. Hard punctuation bans and manufactured personality were not adopted.

Keep attribution in this maintenance reference rather than the active workflow. Do not copy upstream instructions wholesale or reintroduce a second general writing workflow.
